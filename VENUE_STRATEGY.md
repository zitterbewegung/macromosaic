# Publication strategy

## Primary target

**Journal of Applied and Computational Topology (JACT).** The manuscript's strongest fit is the intersection of a discrete topological representation (knot mosaics), an exact computational model, and complexity/algorithmic consequences. The paper should be submitted as an original research article, foregrounding the microscopic construction and the exact topology-to-component-count collapse.

## Strong alternatives

1. **Journal of Knot Theory and Its Ramifications.** Best if the framing emphasizes the fixed-knot extension, mosaic topology, Jones selectors, and the distinction between diagram evaluation and counting completions.
2. **ACM Transactions on Computation Theory.** Best if the framing emphasizes parsimonious completeness, the `SpanP/UP` boundary, parameterized algorithms, and the approximation barrier.
3. **Computational Complexity.** Appropriate but ambitious; the submission should tighten the source-problem reduction and emphasize the exact classification of the unrestricted count.
4. **Theoretical Computer Science.** A broad fallback for the combination of formal language design, counting complexity, and finite algorithms.

## Before submission

- Replace “Anonymous Authors” with names, affiliations, ORCIDs, funding, and acknowledgments.
- Obtain an independent line-by-line proof review of the forced-edge source theorem and the fixed-knot tag-erasure argument.
- Decide whether to keep every generalization in the main article or move the Jones/determinant selectors and width algorithm to an appendix.
- Archive the exact release and add its DOI to the data-and-code statement.
- Check the selected journal's current template and disclosure policy at submission time.

This recommendation is based on journal scope as reviewed on 14 August 2026; it is not a claim of acceptance likelihood.
